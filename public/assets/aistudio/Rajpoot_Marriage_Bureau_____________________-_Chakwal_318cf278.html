About Us

ہمارے بارے میں

Rajpoot Marriage Bureau is based in Main Bazar, Chakwal. We help families find suitable, respectable and well-matched life partners for their sons and daughters.

We have proposals from Pakistan and from overseas, across every sect, caste and community. Whether you are serious about finding a match or worried about a delayed proposal, we are here to help.
